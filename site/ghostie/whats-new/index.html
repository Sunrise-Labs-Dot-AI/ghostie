What's new (v0.13–0.15)

These releases were plumbing. No Don’t Ghost, Birthdays, or Wrapped user-facing ships in this window.

Ghostie now lives at ghostie.app (v0.13.0)

Update feed → https://ghostie.app/appcast.xml. Control / kill-switch manifest → ghostie.app. In-app account, support, terms, and privacy links, plus site metadata, follow. messagesfor.ai stays live as an alias so v0.12 installs keep updating. If you are on v0.11.3 or earlier, auto-update cannot reach you; download the DMG and replace your copy manually (see the v0.12 notes for why).

Remote access from your AI clients (v0.14.0)

Optional: connect a Ghostie account in Settings → Advanced to host a remote MCP from your Mac. Sign up by email and optionally add a passkey. Supported clients can read iMessage and WhatsApp conversations and stage text drafts for your review in Ghostie.

  • You approve every send. Remote access cannot send, approve, schedule, or attach local files.
  • Your Mac is the host. Remote access is unavailable when Ghostie is closed, your Mac sleeps, or the connection drops.
  • Sensitive sign-in content is filtered locally. Ghostie hides suspected verification codes and sign-in links before returning results. Detection may miss unfamiliar formats.
  • Connections use TLS encryption. The relay processes content in memory without intentional message storage or content logging. This is a trusted relay, not end-to-end encryption that hides content from the relay.

Also includes email sign-in fixes and static OAuth support for Claude and Cursor/Grok Bot. In Cursor/Grok Bot, use public client ID ghostie-cursor, scopes messages:read messages:draft, and no client secret. Remote hosting is optional.

Messages opener and Grok Bot link tooling (v0.15.0)

Public mobile Messages opener and a Grok Bot Messages link tool. Relay hardening on top: scope subsets, refresh tokens, queued calls, stale-socket replacement; a heartbeat on the Mac’s relay connection; and a higher per-host request limit for multi-persona clients.
