Little tools for being a better friend

Pick your favorites. Ignore the rest. Ghostie will not take it personally. (It will take it a little personally.)

Don’t Ghost

The conversations that have gone quiet: the ones waiting on your reply, and the ones worth poking first. Sorted by who you’d actually hate to lose. No guilt trips. Okay, mild guilt. Homemade.

Birthdays

Builds a list from people you actually talk to, not your entire Contacts app full of that one realtor from 2019. So you never miss the ones that matter.

Texting Wrapped

Your year in texts as story cards. Equal parts flattering and incriminating. Shareable only if you’re brave.

Texting Analytics

Who texts first? Who double-texts? The receipts, charted, for your eyes only. We would never tell Mom. Unless she asks very nicely.

EQ

A gentle read on your relationships, with tiny nudges to show up better for friends, partner, siblings, the whole circus. Not therapy. Adjacent to therapy. Cheaper than therapy.

Drafts

A calm place to write a text and sit on it, instead of sending something at 2am that becomes a group chat artifact forever.

Scheduled

Line up a text for later, poke yourself to call Mom on Sunday, or set a standing “rent’s due” nudge for your roommate. Future-you can deal with it. Future-you hates you a little.

Work / Personal

Files work threads away from life threads. Severance-mode easter egg included. Your innie would approve. Your outie still texts in meetings.

For the tinkerers (optional, weird, fine)

Want Ghostie to actually ghostwrite? Hand the keys to Claude or Codex and it’ll draft replies in your voice. Every one still waits for your approval: hold to send, like a responsible adult who owns a Mac.

That part’s for people who read READMEs for fun. Everyone else can just… be slightly better at texting.
